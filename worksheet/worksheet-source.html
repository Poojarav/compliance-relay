H. Win time back: tick your biggest loss and its first move

Handoffs: one working group with legal, compliance, security and engineering; interpret in parallel; name partner teams on day one.
Waiting on guidance: build what won't change (inventory, SBOMs, vulnerability intake) while standards settle.
Planning: reserve a standing slice of every planning cycle for regulatory work.
Dependencies: map them, agree dates in writing, escalate any without a committed date.
Capacity: make it an explicit priority with dedicated people; defer lower-value work.
Blocked periods: set milestones before each freeze; treat the last one as your real deadline.
Release lead time: ship compliance on an earlier release train.
Evidence: generate SBOMs, validation results and audit records in the pipeline as you build.

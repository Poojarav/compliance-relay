# Contributing

Thank you for helping improve the Compliance Relay. Contributions of every size are welcome.

## Ways to help

- **Share timeline data.** Open an issue with the "Share anonymized timeline data" template. Ranges are fine. Never share confidential information.
- **Suggest a missing constraint** or point out where the model doesn't match your experience.
- **Improve the calculator, spreadsheet or guide** with a pull request.

## Ground rules

- Use generic, illustrative values only. Do not include information that identifies an organization, product or customer.
- Keep the calculator, spreadsheet and worksheet consistent: a change to the math should be made in all three.
- Be respectful. Assume good intent and keep discussion about the work.
